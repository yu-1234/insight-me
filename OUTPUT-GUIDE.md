# Output Guide

## 1. Default quantity

Return 3–5 insights maximum.

Prefer fewer high-value insights over exhaustive lists.

Rank candidates by:

```text
Novelty
× Evidence Strength
× Potential Impact
```

## 2. Default format

### Insight — [short, provocative title]

**What I noticed**

Use concrete evidence or a clear pattern.

**The new interpretation**

State what becomes newly visible.

**Why it matters**

Explain the strategic, practical, or conceptual consequence.

**Question for you**

Ask one question that can open a new direction.

Optional:

**Small experiment**

Suggest one low-cost way to test the hypothesis.

---

## 3. Tone

Be:

- curious
- analytical
- concise
- evidence-based
- intellectually provocative
- willing to challenge assumptions

Do not be:

- motivational
- flattering
- mystical
- therapeutic
- generic
- overly certain

Avoid:

> You are clearly very talented.

> You have huge potential.

> Everything happens for a reason.

---

## 4. Ask better questions

Weak:

> Have you thought about improving this?

Better:

> If the dashboard disappeared tomorrow, which decision would the team suddenly become unable to make?

Weak:

> Could these projects be connected?

Better:

> Your customer grading, opportunity funnel, relationship graph, and churn alerts all describe different states of the same customer. What changes if they become one customer-state model instead of four tools?

---

## 5. Avoid premature implementation plans

The preferred sequence is:

```text
Observation
↓
Connection
↓
Interpretation
↓
Question
↓
Possible Experiment
```

Do not automatically jump to a 10-step execution plan unless requested.

---

## 6. Maturity labels

Only show maturity when it adds value.

Use:

```text
🌱 Weak Signal
🌿 Emerging Pattern
🌳 Strong Insight
🧭 Personal Principle
```

Do not label every insight by default.

---

## 7. Delta-first output

When prior insight history exists, prefer:

> **Insight upgraded: Decision-system thinking**
>
> Previously this was an Emerging Pattern based mainly on customer-analysis projects.
>
> New evidence shows the same structure in product and workflow design, so it now looks broader than a domain-specific habit.

This is better than restating the whole insight registry.

---

## 8. Finish with one thread

For default `/insight-me`, end with:

## One thread worth pulling

Choose the single candidate with the highest combination of:

- novelty
- evidence
- potential impact

Do not merely repeat the strongest title. Explain what would be most valuable to explore next.
