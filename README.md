# insight-me

> Memory tells you what happened.
> Reflection tells you what it meant.
> Insight reveals what you still haven't noticed.

**insight-me** is an Agent Skill that turns your past conversations
and experiences into new insights.

It does not simply summarize your history.

Instead, it looks for:

- Hidden recurring patterns
- Unexpected connections
- Contradictions
- Unconscious problem-solving methods
- Missing implications
- Abandoned ideas
- Second-order effects
- Blind spots
- New hypotheses

## The difference

### Memory

"You previously built customer segmentation."

### Reuse

"You could reuse customer segmentation in another project."

### insight-me

"Customer segmentation, opportunity funnels, churn alerts and
relationship graphs may actually be different dimensions of a
single Customer State model."

That's the goal:

> **Connect things from your past that you had never connected yourself.**
